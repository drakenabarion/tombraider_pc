Credits
=======

Cyber Troopers Virtual-On Masterpiece 1995~2001, native port by drakenabarion.

Lineage: bbport (Bloodborne on Linux, deadinside28, github.com/deadinside28/bloodborne_pc)
-> its Windows port (SuperMedo, github.com/Supermedo/bloodborne_pc)
-> uncharted_pc (Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection, SuperMedo, github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc)
-> this port. The renderer is shadPS4's video core (github.com/shadps4-emu/shadPS4).
Video decoding (Videodec2) and the DLSS bridge come from IFreemz/shadPS4-Bloodborne-DLSS-FSR.
Some names in the port (files, settings, parts of the in-game overlay) still come from the
Bloodborne and Uncharted ports it is built on.

Also used:
- NGS2 sound: the NGS2 implementation of AnyPS5 (github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5, GPL-2.0)
- ATRAC9 decoding: LibAtrac9 (github.com/Thealexbarney/LibAtrac9)
- FSR-Vulkan (github.com/FireBurn/FSR-Vulkan) and the AMD FidelityFX SDK; FSR 4 assets from
  FireBurn/Q2RTX
- NVIDIA DLSS SDK (Linux builds made with it; NVIDIA's libraries under NVIDIA's license)
- SDL3, FFmpeg, Dear ImGui, sirit, magic_enum, xbyak, Vulkan Memory Allocator, LLVM,
  PyInstaller, Pillow and the Cinzel font (SIL Open Font License)
